Wi-Fi Connection Guide

How to Connect to Wi-Fi

1

Enable Wi-Fi on Your Device

macOS: Click the Wi-Fi icon in the menu bar and select "Turn Wi-Fi On"

Windows: Go to Settings → Network & Internet → Wi-Fi → Turn on

2

Scan for Available Networks

Click the "Scan Now" button in the dashboard to scan for nearby Wi-Fi networks.

The system will automatically detect all available networks and display them with signal strength.

3

View Network Details

Check the "Detected Networks" section to see:

  • Network name (SSID)
  • Signal strength (RSSI in dBm)
  • Signal quality (Excellent/Good/Fair/Poor)
  • Coverage range (up to 10 meters)
4

Connect to a Network

macOS:

  1. Click the Wi-Fi icon in the menu bar
  2. Select the network you want to connect to
  3. Enter the password if required

Windows:

  1. Click the Wi-Fi icon in the system tray
  2. Select your network from the list
  3. Click "Connect" and enter the password
5

Monitor Connection Quality

After connecting, use the dashboard to monitor:

  • Signal Quality: Real-time assessment of your connection
  • Location Accuracy: Your position based on Wi-Fi fingerprinting
  • Signal Chart: Historical signal strength trends

Tips for Better Wi-Fi Performance

  • Excellent Signal (-30 to -50 dBm): Optimal for all applications
  • Good Signal (-50 to -60 dBm): Good for most activities
  • Fair Signal (-60 to -70 dBm): May experience slower speeds
  • Poor Signal (below -70 dBm): Consider moving closer to the router

Understanding the Map

  • The map shows your current location with an accuracy circle
  • Wi-Fi networks are displayed with colored range circles (up to 10 meters)
  • Green circles = Excellent signal, Blue = Good, Yellow = Fair, Red = Poor
  • Click on network markers to see detailed information
